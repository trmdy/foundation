Apiary — Account usage

A fleet of agents spends one shared, invisible resource: provider quota. This pane makes it visible — every account, both rolling windows, and the one number the CLI cannot show you in a table: where the current burn rate lands you before the window resets.

TWO WINDOWS, NOT ONE NUMBER A 5h window and a weekly window fail differently: the 5h one strands a bee mid-task and refills by dinner; the weekly one ends your week. They get equal width because neither is the summary of the other.
PACE IS THE SIGNAL Used percent alone says nothing — 58% is fine four hours in and alarming forty minutes in. Pace is used% minus elapsed%, so the sign is the whole message: negative is headroom, positive is a window you will not finish.
COLOUR IS RATIONED Fifteen of seventeen accounts are grey. Tone only leaves calm when the projection crosses 100% — that is the whole colour budget, and it is why the two rows that matter find you instead of the other way round.
UNREACHABLE ≠ IDLE An account with no OAuth token has no usage, not zero usage. It keeps its row and carries the CLI's own error text verbatim, because the fix is a re-auth, not a rebalance.
Two accounts report no usage at all — one has no OAuth token in the vault, one returned no plan. Their rows stay in place, greyed, with the error verbatim: quota you cannot see is not quota you have.
